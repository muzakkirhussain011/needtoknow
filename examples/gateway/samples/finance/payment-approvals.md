# Payment approval limits

Payments up to SAR 50,000 need one approver from treasury. Payments from SAR 50,000 to SAR 500,000 need two approvers, one of them the treasury lead. Payments above SAR 500,000 also need CFO approval.

# Vendor bank details

New vendor bank accounts are verified by a call-back to a phone number already on file, never to a number supplied in the request. The primary operating account for the fictional company is IBAN SA4420000001234567891234.

# Emergency payments

Emergency payments outside the weekly run need a written reason and are reviewed by internal audit every month.
