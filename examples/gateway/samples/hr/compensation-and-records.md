# Compensation bands (2026)

Band L2 (associate): SAR 9,000 to 13,000 per month. Band L3 (engineer): SAR 13,000 to 19,000 per month. Band L4 (senior): SAR 19,000 to 27,000 per month. Housing allowance is 25% of basic salary and transport allowance is 10%.

Salary reviews happen every March. Out-of-cycle increases need approval from the HR director and the CFO.

# Updating an employee record

Employee record changes are made in the HR system by the HR business partner. Never send these details by email; share them only through the HR system.

# Probation

Probation lasts 90 days and can be extended once by up to 90 more days with written notice to the employee.
