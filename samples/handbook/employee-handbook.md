# Working at Najm Logistics

Najm Logistics is a fictional regional freight company used to demonstrate the gateway. Core hours are 9:00 to 15:00, Sunday to Thursday. Hybrid work is allowed two days a week with your manager's agreement.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 21 working days of annual leave per year, rising to 30 days after five years of service. Leave requests go through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Up to 5 unused days can be carried into the next year.

## Sick leave

Sick leave longer than two consecutive days needs a medical certificate uploaded to the HR portal within three working days.

## Expense claims

Business expenses are claimed in the finance portal within 30 days, with receipts attached. Claims above SAR 5,000 need the department head's approval before submission.

## Information security basics

Never share passwords or one-time codes. Report suspected phishing to the security team. Customer data must stay inside approved company systems and must never be pasted into public AI tools.
